Agentic Design 0.6.1: a better chat with the agent.
Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. Installed copies update themselves.

## New
- The agent's answers are formatted: headings, lists, bold, code and links.
  The agent can link to a layer or a place on the canvas, and show a live picture of a layer right in the chat. A click brings it into view.
- See what the agent did: open any tool call to read its input and result.
- The context meter in the chat's header shows how full the agent's context is. Click it to compact the conversation into a summary and go on.
- Select text in the chat and quote it in your reply from the right-click menu.
- Drag the edge of the agent's panel to make it wider.

## Improved
- ⌥⌫ deletes a word and ⌘⌫ the line, in the chat and every field; ⌥ and ⌘ with the arrows move by words and lines.
- Right-click menus with cut, copy, paste and select all in the chat field.
- Fewer sparkles around the interface.

## Fixed
- Spaces at the end of what you type now show.
- The chat scrolls in the trackpad's direction.
