Agentic Design 0.1.3: open .pen files from Finder, and vector PDF from the Export panel.

- **Open from Finder.** Double-clicking a `.pen` file, Open With, dropping one on the Dock
  icon or `open file.pen` opens it in a tab (or brings up the tab that has it), with its
  libraries. Before, macOS said the app could not open files in the "Pen document" format.
  `.pen` files are now a declared document type that Quick Look and Spotlight read as JSON.
- **Vector PDF from the Export panel.** Exporting a layer as PDF writes real paths and
  selectable, searchable text; with nothing selected, every frame of the page becomes a
  page of one PDF. Blurs are not drawn yet in PDF, and gradients and shadows are images.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Signed with a Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. Installed copies update themselves.
