Agentic Design 0.1.3: open .pen files from Finder, and vector PDF from the Export panel.
Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. Installed copies update themselves.

## Improved
- PDF from the Export panel is vector, with selectable, searchable text.
  With nothing selected, every frame of the page becomes a page of one PDF. Blurs are not drawn in PDF yet, and gradients and shadows are images.
- .pen files are a declared document type.
  Quick Look and Spotlight read them as JSON.

## Fixed
- Opening a .pen file from Finder works.
  Double-click, Open With, a drop on the Dock icon or `open file.pen` opens it in a tab with its libraries; macOS no longer says the app cannot open "Pen document" files.
