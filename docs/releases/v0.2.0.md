Agentic Design 0.2.0: a new look, with more room for the canvas. Requires a Mac with Apple
Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Installed
copies update themselves.

## New
- Floating panels.
  Layers and the inspector float over the canvas, which now fills the window. Hide either with the button in its header, or both with ⌘\; a hidden panel leaves a small button in its corner that shows where it opens.
- The interface comes in four sizes.
  Settings → General: 90, 100, 115 or 130%, or ⌘⌥+ and ⌘⌥− from anywhere (⌘⌥0 goes back to 100%). Panels and text grow; the canvas keeps its zoom.
- New Settings.
  Sections for your account, general, canvas, agents and updates. Sign in to the cloud from Account.

## Improved
- The tools are in a bar at the bottom of the canvas; zoom, Export and Share are in the tab bar.
  Double click a tab to rename its file.
- A cleaner Dashboard.
  One header with All files and Recents, search, sort, New file, Settings and your account; the cards fill the window.
- Previews of very tall or wide pages stay readable.
  They show the top of the page instead of all of it tiny.

## Fixed
- What's new opened from Settings no longer closes at once.
