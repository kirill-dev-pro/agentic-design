Agentic Design 0.1.5: every design on your Mac on the Dashboard, and Share by link. Requires a
Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and notarized by
Apple. Installed copies update themselves.

## New
- The Dashboard lists every .pen file on your Mac.
  All files finds them with Spotlight and a quiet scan of your home folder, newest first; sort by last modified, last opened, name or created, and search by name. Recents keeps the files you opened in the app.
- Share a file by link.
  Share at the top of the inspector signs you in through the browser (no password in the app), uploads the file with its images, fonts and libraries, and copies a link that opens in any browser. Upload changes keeps the same link; download and embedding are optional, and the link can be turned off.
- Move to Trash from the Dashboard.
  Right-click a card or press Delete; it asks first, and the file can be put back from the Trash. Remove from list and Hide this folder only hide files.

## Improved
- Previews appear by themselves.
  Cards without a preview get one in the background, and a preview is redrawn when its file changes.
- What's new speaks Russian on a Mac set to Russian.
