Agentic Design 0.6.2: pictures for the agent, updates you see, agents you can watch.
Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. Installed copies update themselves.

## New
- Paste a picture into the agent's field (⌘V), or drop image files on its panel.
  They go with your next request only, and stay in the conversation with it. Other files you drop are attached as context.
- See when an agent works on a file: a spinner in its tab and a glowing card on the Dashboard.
- A new version downloads by itself: a Restart to update button appears at the top right.

## Improved
- The app looks for updates every hour instead of once a day.
- The Dashboard opens on Recents.
- The agent picker says Default when the agent uses its own model and reasoning.

## Fixed
- A click on a tool call in the conversation no longer selects text.
