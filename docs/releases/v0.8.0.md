Agentic Design 0.8.0: cloud files open live, in the app and in the browser, with cursors, comments and three levels of access.
The macOS app requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; it is signed with a
Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and installed copies update themselves. Windows and
Linux builds are previews. Downloads: https://agentic-design.kcrz.dev/download/ ·
the cloud: https://agentic-design.kcrz.dev/cloud/

## New
- Cloud files open live.
  Open a file from your dashboard on the website with Open in app, or in the browser: everyone in it sees each other's cursors and changes as they happen. Undo takes back only your own steps. Live editing in the app is on macOS; on Windows and Linux, open cloud files in the browser.
- Comments in threads on cloud files.
  The comment button beside the tool bar (or ⇧C) pins a comment to a point of the design; others reply under it. Change or delete your own; editors and the owner delete any and resolve threads.
- Three levels of access: view, comment, edit.
  The owner invites people by email from the dashboard's People and changes their access at any time; it takes effect at once, even in an open file.

## Improved
- A comment mark of Agent mode opens again with a click, to change its text; its card has a Delete button.

## Fixed
- Dictation apps and text expanders paste again: ⌘V that comes as the key's flags alone (no press of ⌘) works in comments, the agent's field and every other field.
