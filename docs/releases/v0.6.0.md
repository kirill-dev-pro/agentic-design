Agentic Design 0.6.0: an Agent mode, real text editing, and models to choose.
Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. Installed copies update themselves.

## New
- Design and Agent modes.
  The switch at the left of the tab bar. In Agent the agent's panel docks at the left: your sessions, the conversation and the input; the bar at the bottom of the canvas is for comments, pens, arrows, areas and tags for the agent. ⌘L opens it.
- Pick the model and reasoning.
  The agent menu lists each agent's models (Codex's from its own list) and the reasoning effort, from Low to Max. Your choice is remembered.
- Edit text on the canvas.
  Click to place the caret, drag or double click to select, use the arrows with ⌥ and ⌘, copy, cut and paste.
- Copy to cloud from the Dashboard, and Duplicate and Rename for any file.
- An empty file shows the main shortcuts; all of them are in Settings › Canvas.

## Improved
- Comments for the agent look as designed and wrap their text.
- Text from the agent's conversation can be selected and copied.
- A copied text layer pastes as plain text in other apps, and text from elsewhere pastes as a text layer.
- The tab bar keeps only the zoom; export is in the canvas's right-click menu and ⇧⌘E.

## Fixed
- ⌘, opens Settings on the Dashboard.
