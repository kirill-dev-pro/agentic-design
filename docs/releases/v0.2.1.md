Agentic Design 0.2.1: opens on the Dashboard, and Settings stays open. Requires a Mac with
Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
Installed copies update themselves.

## Improved
- The app opens on the Dashboard.
  Starting it from the Dock or Launchpad shows your files instead of a new empty one; files opened from Finder still open in tabs.

## Fixed
- Settings, Share and Keyboard shortcuts no longer close as soon as they open.
  The click on the button that opened them counted as a click outside them.
