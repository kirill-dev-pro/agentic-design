Agentic Design 0.3.1: agents can share your files, when you allow it. Requires a Mac with
Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
Installed copies update themselves.

## New
- Agents can put a file in your cloud and share it.
  Ask an agent to upload a design: it stays private, visible only to you, unless you allow a link. Before a link is made, the app asks you.
- Agents can invite a person to a file.
  As a viewer or an editor, after you say yes in the app. The agent gives you the invitation link to pass on; the person opens it signed in with that email. Agents can also list who has access and take it away.
