      How it works

      Four things to know.

      1. Your cloud files

        Put a .pen in the cloud from the Mac app (Copy to cloud, or Share) or from the viewer here. It is private: only you see it until you invite someone or make a link.

      2. Open it live

        Open a file from here in the browser, or in the app with Open in app. Everyone in it sees each other's cursors and changes as they happen.

      3. Invite people

        A file's People: an email and Can view, Can comment or Can edit. They open the invitation signed in with that address. You can change their access or remove them at any time.

      4. Comment

        The comment button beside the tool bar pins a comment to a point of the design. Others reply under it; the author, an editor or you resolve it.

      People

      Invite someone by email. They open the invitation signed in with that address. Can view: looks and follows along. Can comment: also leaves comments and replies. Can edit: also changes the design, live with everyone in it.

        Links

        Anyone with a link can view the file, without an account. Revoking a link stops it at once.
