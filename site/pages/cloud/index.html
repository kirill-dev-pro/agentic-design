Design together, live

Put a .pen in the Agentic Design cloud and open it in the app or in your browser. Everyone in it sees each other's cursors and changes as they happen, and what your agent changes reaches them too. Invite people to view, comment or edit.

Your own files stay where they are: a file goes to the cloud only when you put it there.

Live, with cursors

Edits reach everyone at once, and everyone's pointer is on the canvas with their name. Two people change different things and both changes stay; undo takes back only your own steps.

In the app or the browser

The same editor either way. Open a file from your dashboard in the browser, or press Open in app. Agents connected to the Mac app work on cloud files too: their changes reach everyone, like yours.

Comments in threads

Pin a comment to any point of the design. Others reply under it; whoever started the thread, an editor or the owner resolves it when it is done.

Private by default

A cloud file is yours until you invite someone by email, or make a view link. Removing someone, or changing their access, takes effect at once, even mid-session.

How it works

Three steps

  1. Put a file in the cloud. In the Mac app, Copy to cloud on its Dashboard (or Share on an open file). In the browser, open the file in the viewer and press Share.
  2. Invite people. On your dashboard, a file's People: an email and Can view, Can comment or Can edit. They get a link to open signed in with that address.
  3. Open it together. Everyone opens the file from their dashboard, in the browser or in the app, and works on it at the same time.

The browser editor runs on WebGPU: Chrome and Edge 113 or later, Safari 26 or later, Firefox 141 or later on Windows. Live editing in the app is on macOS for now; on Windows and Linux, open cloud files in the browser.
